## About DecaFlow

DecaFlow is a Web3 infrastructure company providing compliance, security, and risk intelligence solutions for crypto-native businesses, exchanges, fintech companies, and institutional investors. Our products help companies stay compliant with global regulations, protect their smart contracts, and screen wallets for sanctions, fraud, and illicit activity.

We're building the compliance and security layer for the next generation of finance.

---

## The Role

We're hiring Business Development Executives to drive enterprise sales and partnerships. You'll be responsible for identifying, engaging, and closing deals with crypto exchanges, DeFi protocols, fintech companies, institutional funds, and RWA (Real-World Asset) issuers who need compliance and security infrastructure.

This is a performance-based role. Your base salary of $2,000/month is paid when you meet the monthly sales target of $15,000–$20,000 in new revenue.

---

## What You'll Do

- **Prospect and outreach:** Identify and reach out to potential customers — exchanges, protocols, fintechs, funds, and RWA issuers
- **Run sales cycles:** From first call to signed contract, own the full sales process
- **Demo our products:** Present DecaFlow's Verify API, Shield, Compliance, Autopilot, Audit, and Institutional solutions
- **Close deals:** Negotiate and close contracts worth $2,500–$50,000+/month
- **Build relationships:** Develop long-term partnerships with enterprise clients
- **Hit targets:** Consistently close $15,000–$20,000+ in new monthly revenue
